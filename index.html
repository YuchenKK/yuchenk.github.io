Ph.D. Student in Mechanical Engineering
University of Massachusetts Amherst

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. My research focuses on soft robotics, compliant mechanisms, and mechanically intelligent structures.

My work explores the design of soft robotic systems, pneumatic soft actuators, variable stiffness, and structural reinforcement for soft robots.

Soft Robotic Arms

Soft Actuators

Variable Stiffness

Mechanical approaches for controlling and increasing the stiffness of soft robotic systems without sacrificing their useful deformation modes.

Compliant Mechanical Structures
